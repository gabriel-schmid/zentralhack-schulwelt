# Concept notes (Friday evening)

Transcribed from our handwritten notes. German terms kept as written, English
next to them. Words marked (?) were hard to read.

## What works in today's school (+)

- Lernen, Durchmischung – learning, mixing of ages/groups
- Klassen, **Zugehörigkeit** – classes, belonging
- Respekt gegenüber LP, Vorbildfunktion – respect towards teachers, role models
- Viele Fächer / Abwechslung – many subjects, variety
- **Klare Struktur** – clear structure
- Feedbackkultur – feedback culture

## What is missing (−)

- Keine/wenig innere Differenzierung – little or no differentiation within a class
- Praxisbezug: «Wieso lerne ich das?» – practical relevance: "why am I learning this?"
- Kein Platz für Interesse / Wahlmöglichkeiten – no room for interests or choice

## Core idea

**Raum für intrinsische Motivation** – room for intrinsic motivation, held by:

- Struktur: Zeit, Räume, Personen/Coaches – structure: time, rooms, people/coaches
- Überprüfbarkeit – it can be checked
- Abwechslung – variety
- Mindestanforderung / LP – minimum requirements, set with the teacher
- Verbindlichkeit – commitment

## Dashboard / Überblick (overview)

- Briefing vom Tag – briefing of the day
- 1on1 planbar – 1:1 sessions can be booked
- Abschluss / Erzählen / Highlights – closing the day: telling, highlights
- Freitag: 1on1, Feedback – Friday: 1:1 and feedback
- A timeline with milestones (progress path)

## Building blocks

- **NPC prompting** ← Vorlage (template) + Personas
- **Lehrer-Persona** – teacher persona
- **Besonderes Interesse** – special interest (room for choice)
- **Rahmenbedingungen** (constraints): zeitliche Struktur (time structure),
  Räume (rooms), Lehrplan/Lernziel (curriculum, learning goal)
- **Formatives Assessment → summatives Assessment** – formative feedback along
  the way leading to a summative assessment
