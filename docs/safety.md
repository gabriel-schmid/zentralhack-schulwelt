# Safety in the world

Safety is a natural part of the projects, not a theme of its own. Two or three
moments per place are enough.

| Place | Moment |
| ----- | ------ |
| Werkstatt (workshop) | Pick safety glasses and ear protection before sawing; spot a cable on the floor and a missing saw guard as hazards |
| Garten und Hof (garden and yard) | Lift heavy sacks correctly; put garden tools down safely |
| Küche (kitchen) | Hold and put down knives correctly; hot pans; wash hands |

Take facts and rules from official material (e.g. Suva), never invent them.
Put the source next to the rule.

| Rule in the widget | Source |
| ------------------ | ------ |
| Wear safety glasses | chemsuisse 2019, 2.4.3 (safety glasses while experimenting; adjustable glasses with side protection recommended) and Anhang A |
| Sunglasses are no substitute («Sie schützt nicht vor Spritzern von der Seite.») | Suva Augenschutz: ordinary glasses have «keinerlei Schutzwirkung», only professional eye protection protects; chemsuisse 2019, 2.4.3 recommends side protection. Neither source names sunglasses explicitly; the widget applies the general statement about ordinary glasses. |
| Wear gloves | chemsuisse 2019, 2.4.3 (nitrile or neoprene against acids and bases; disposable gloves for single splashes) and Anhang A («wo nötig auch die Handschuhe») |
| Never taste | chemsuisse 2019, Anhang A: no taste tests («kein Probieren») |
| No Znüni at the bench | chemsuisse 2019, 2.4.1 and Anhang A: eating, drinking and keeping food in the lab are forbidden |
| Do not sniff at the beaker | chemsuisse 2019, Anhang A: smell only when the teacher asks. The earlier wording «Höchstens Luft zufächeln» was dropped: no Swiss official source for wafting was found (only German state school rules). |
| Wash hands afterwards (closing message) | chemsuisse 2019, Anhang A: wash hands after experimenting and tidying up |

Suva factsheet 33107.D «Sicherer Umgang mit Chemikalien» (2024) was checked;

school or bench rules, so it is not cited.
