# Safety in the world

Safety is a natural part of the projects, not a theme of its own. Two or three
moments per place are enough.

| Place | Moment |
| ----- | ------ |
| Werkstatt (workshop) | Pick safety glasses and ear protection before sawing; spot a cable on the floor and a missing saw guard as hazards |
| Garten und Hof (garden and yard) | Lift heavy sacks correctly; put garden tools down safely |
| Küche (kitchen) | Hold and put down knives correctly; hot pans; wash hands |

Take facts and rules from official material (e.g. Suva), never invent them.
Put the source next to the rule.
