# Pedagogical principles

What makes this school. The AI coach and the NPCs know these principles
([coach/system-prompt.md](../coach/system-prompt.md)). Written as our own
vision, not as a quote from a real school. Source:
[concept-notes.md](concept-notes.md).

## Core idea

**Room for intrinsic motivation inside a clear structure.** Learners follow
their own interests and choose their work, but within a frame that keeps it
binding.

## What we keep from today's school

- **Belonging:** classes and groups you belong to, mixed ages and backgrounds.
- **Clear structure:** a known rhythm for the day and the week.
- **Role models:** teachers are respected and set an example.
- **Variety:** many subjects, different kinds of work.
- **Feedback culture:** feedback is normal and frequent.

## What we add

- **Differentiation:** each learner works at their own level and pace.
- **Practical relevance:** every task answers "why am I learning this?",
  ideally through a real project.
- **Choice and special interests:** time and a place for what a learner
  cares about.

## The frame that holds it

| Element | In the world |
| ------- | ------------ |
| Time structure | Tagesplan: Morgenrunde, fixed and free blocks, Abendrunde; Friday 1:1 and feedback |
| Rooms | Each house is a place with its own purpose and interior |
| People / coaches | Coach, teacher personas and other NPCs |
| Curriculum / learning goals | Minimum requirements agreed with the teacher |
| Checkability | Milestones on the progress path |
| Commitment | Goals set in the 1:1 are followed up |

## Assessment

- **Formative**, along the way: coach conversations, Friday 1:1, feedback at
  the stations.
- **Summative:** still open. How individual summative evidence works when
  learning happens in group projects is not decided; a project presentation
  alone is not yet an agreed assessment model
  ([shared-concept.md](shared-concept.md), open decision 6). The existing
  grading regulations (Notenreglement) continue to apply.

## Responsibility

For yourself, for others and for the animals and plants in the village.
Working safely is part of that ([safety.md](safety.md)).
