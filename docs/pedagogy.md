# Pedagogical principles

What makes this school. The AI coach knows these principles
([coach/system-prompt.md](../coach/system-prompt.md)). Written as our own
vision, not as a quote from a real school.

- **Independence:** learners plan their week and their projects themselves.
- **1:1 coaching:** regular conversations to set goals and look at where you
  stand.
- **Learning in real projects:** you build, tend and cook things that are
  actually needed.
- **Reflection:** after each project you look back: what went well, what will
  I do differently next time?
- **Responsibility:** for yourself, for others and for the animals and plants
  in the village. Working safely is part of that.
