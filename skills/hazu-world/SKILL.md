# Hazu World

Hazu World is a **view** on a Hazu, like the list or the calendar. The data
are ordinary Hazu items; the world only renders them.

## Hazu tree → world

| On hazu.app | In the world |
| ----------- | ------------ |
| World Hazu (`layout.defaultView: "world"`) | the village |
| Sub-Hazu inside the world Hazu | a house with a door; walking in opens that place |
| Punkt (item) | a character or board with text, images or a quiz |
| Hazu with tag `hz-world-sim` and a widget | a station; opens the widget as an overlay |
| Genius configuration in «Figuren» (tag `hazuworld-characters`) | a tutor character, e.g. the coach |

As long as no level has been saved, the engine generates a village from the
sub-Hazus and Punkte automatically.

## Level builder

- Opens in the app's world view, for editors of the Hazu only.
- Saving creates a new version of the level document. «Versionen» brings back
  earlier versions.
- **Only one person in the builder at a time.** The level is one document; the
  last save wins.
- Export bigger versions to `level/` as a backup and commit them.

## Our level

- The village is `level/bergdorf.json`, generated by `node level/build-bergdorf.mjs`.
  The world Hazu is its own level (like Alpdorf); its pin lives in
  `layout.world` of `TRAIMeIVPNXq5wfkfTG2`.
- If someone saves in the builder, the builder's version becomes the newest
  revision. Then export it to `level/` before regenerating from the script, or
  the script overwrites the builder's changes.
- Open-air rooms on the same island: `rendererFn: "forumTheatre"`
  (amphitheatre, morning and evening circle) and `rendererFn: "auditorium"`
  (seats, screen, lecterns; workshops). True walk-in interiors don't exist;
  a building with `hazuId` is a door that opens that place as its own island.
- Own buildings (e.g. open-sided school halls, work bays) can be written as
  `hazu.world/landmark@1` documents carried in the level
  (`data.landmarks`), no engine change needed. The Werkstatt-Welt does this.
- 3D models (`.glb`) only appear as exhibits: a Punkt with
  `<model-viewer src="….glb">` stands as a turning model on a plinth. Not
  usable as walk-in buildings.
- **Interiors** are their own level, pinned on the world (`layout.world.levels`)
  with the place's `hazuId`. Their exit is a building with
  `isTravelPoint: true` and `destination: "Bergdorf"` (the level's name). Never
  `hazu:<world id>`: a door to the world Hazu opens a new app tab. Places
  without their own level get an auto back door that has exactly that
  problem.
- NPCs: `dialogue[npcId]` holds intro, hint and quiz phases; `isGenius: true`
  plus `geniusSystemPrompt` makes an AI character.

## Who can change what

- **All editors**, in the browser: places (sub-Hazus), character content
  (Punkte), texts, quizzes, the builder.
- **Gabriel**: world settings, uploading widgets and wiring them in as
  stations.

## Uploading a level revision

The level is a managed file (`level.json`) on its place Hazu; the world pins
one revision. For a small change, patch the live revision with the Hazu MCP
`patch_file_source` (exact find/replace) instead of re-sending the whole file
as base64: a hand-copied 50 KB base64 once changed one word in a dialogue.
Either way, the returned sha256 must equal the local file's before you move
the world's pin (`layout.world.levels[]`, only that entry).

Always upload the converted document, never the raw `level/*.json`: run
`doc-level.mjs` (the engine's `levelRoundTrip`), which adds
`"$schema": "hazu.world/level@1"` and `version`. A raw file uploads fine but
the engine rejects it («pinned document … is no level@1») and shows a generic
fallback village instead of our level. After every upload, open the world and
check the console for that warning.

## Playing and testing

- **Interact with X or Enter** (or click), not E. Q/E turn the camera.
- **Use the fullscreen button** (top right in the world). In the normal app
  view the world can be taller than the window: dialogue text, the «Weiter»
  button and door hints end up below the visible area, and hint labels may
  flicker. In fullscreen everything shows.
- Doors sit on the side of a house facing the road; walk there and press X.

## Limits (as of October 2026)

- Quiz answers given in the world are not saved yet.
- VR and voice only work in the standalone tab, and there without Genius. The
  demo runs in a laptop browser.
- People who are not editors of the Hazu only see the world once it is
  switched on for readers.
